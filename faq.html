What is UGC (user-generated content) and how is it different from influencer marketing?

UGC is short-form, native-feeling video and photo content shot to look like an everyday customer or creator filmed it themselves, rather than a polished brand ad. Unlike traditional influencer marketing, UGC is not usually posted on the creator’s own social account to their followers — it’s delivered to the brand as a raw asset for the brand to run as paid social ads or organic content on its own channels.

What does CG Showcase create content for?

Cerys, the creator behind CG Showcase, creates realistic, aspirational UGC and content for product brands, hospitality (hotels, dining and activities) and travel and experience brands, produced for TikTok, Instagram and paid social.

What UGC formats and styles does Cerys shoot?

For product and service brands: Fun Edits (viral-format and unexpected-product hooks, product-as-character, stop motion), Lifestyle-Integrated content (product reviews, day-in-the-life, trust-building narratives), Direct Response formats (demo and how-to, problem to solution, the upgrade), Couples content, and comedy Skits. For hospitality and travel brands: dining and activities content, and hotel content. Cerys also shoots on-location photography.

What packages are available?
